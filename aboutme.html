Photography

I have been a photographer for about a decade now. Back when I started, I took pictures on my Samsung Galaxy Prevail LTE (one of these pictures in the gallery was taken on that Galaxy). And it wasn't until my sophomore year of high school, when I saved up enough to buy my first camera. Since then, it's been a trusty companion that I have to count on. It has traveled with me from high school to college, from Mexico to England. It has been by my side always, ready to capture everlasting memories. Included on this page are just some favorite pictures I have taken. Lately, a majority of what I have come to love taking pictures of are land/cityscapes. Which is a stark contrast to what I used to take all the time, portraits. On my Instagram there are a lot more portraits that I have shot, however for the purposes of this website, I tried to stick strictly to only land/cityscapes. I do have some portraits, that I am particularly proud of.

Tech Tinkerer

Lately I have started playing around and tinkering with small computers, specifically Raspberry Pi's and Arduinos. Messing around with these different types of hardware has allowed me to learn a lot about writing code for it. Right now I'm working on a plant project which would involve setting up an arduino with a photoresistor, soil moisture sensor, and a humidity sensor. These sensors would work together to track the plants health and suggest optimal times to water said plants. One project that I have finished is turning a Raspberry Pi into a network wide ad blocker. This allows anyone on my home network to be able to view news articles, shop, watch videos, all without ads constantly being brought up. I eventually want to also give it VPN capabilities.

Legos & Model Kits

Another interest that I have is building stuff. Legos were the first toys that sparked my imagination for creating things. From there things only too off. To this day I still enjoy playing around with LEGOs and creating random creatures or rebuilding old sets from my childhood. Along with LEGOs, I have also recently got into model kits, specifically Gundam. Although I haven't watched the Gundam anime, I love how the kits look and how they come together. The building process that these kits take allows me to learn to take things slow and to look at things from different angles.
